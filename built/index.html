FAQ

Questions

Who can use hax.psu.edu?

Anyone with a Penn State account: log in and start building. HAX itself is free and open source, so anyone outside Penn State can get it from haxtheweb.org.

How are decisions made?

By the people who use it: students, faculty and staff who take or build courses with HAX. The Core Product Development team meets monthly and is open to any group contributing development, design or other skills. To join, contact Bill Rose, Product Owner (bmr1@psu.edu).

Why was HAX created?

A university Request for Information sought a custom instructional content management system. The requirements went far beyond what any vendor offered and no vendor answered the request for proposal, so Penn State built a new kind of solution.
