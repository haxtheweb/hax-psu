What is HAX?

A standards-based publishing platform for web authoring and content ownership.

HAX is Penn State's free, open-source web authoring platform for accessible, standards-based publishing. It helps teams publish faster without sacrificing quality, through portable content and long-term ownership that is not tied to a single vendor.

HAX supports teaching, research, outreach and student development with a shared platform that scales from one course site to institution-wide initiatives. The platform is powered by HAXcms, originally developed to power hundreds of high-scale online courses.

Get started

  • Log in with your Penn State account to create a site on HAX at Penn State.
  • Try the HAX editor without logging in.
  • Read the HAX documentation.
  • Prefer the command line? Run npm install --global @haxtheweb/create then hax start.

Built for every role at Penn State

Faculty

Create and update accessible course materials quickly. Reuse content across semesters, keep ownership of your work, and focus on teaching instead of platform complexity.

Students

Build portfolios, resumes and project sites you can take with you beyond graduation. HAX is portable and lightweight, and gives students meaningful ways to learn by doing while contributing to a vibrant campus publishing community.

Staff

Build a department website, a project website, or a personal website for a short-term initiative. Set up a quick site about a project, download the site and host it on another server, or host content from GitHub or anywhere else.

Institutional impact

HAX combines academic flexibility with operational discipline. It is supported through cross-college collaboration and designed for long-term maintainability.

  • Open-source and portable architecture that reduces vendor lock-in risk
  • Accessibility-forward publishing practices
  • Shared governance across participating units
  • Demonstrated adoption across courses, programs and public-facing sites

Frequently asked questions

Who can use HAX?

Anyone, anywhere. HAX is open source, so all you need is a web server and a domain name to get started. It also supports publishing directly to GitHub Pages and other static hosts. If you are part of Penn State, just log in to get started immediately.

Who supports HAX?

At Penn State, HAX is supported by a collaboration between the College of Arts and Architecture, the College of Information Sciences and Technology, the College of Education, the Eberly College of Science and University Libraries. HAX also receives contributions from groups outside Penn State, including Buttercups LLC and community members at large.

How do I join the community?

Join the HAX community Discord to get support from the community and the HAX team.

Why was HAX created?

HAX responds to a lack of products that meet the need for accessible, engaging web content in online course environments. It was born out of a university Request for Information (RFI) for a custom Drupal instructional content management system. The requirements gathered across the university went far beyond what any commercial product offered, and no vendors responded to the subsequent Request for Proposal, so the university built a new kind of solution.

How are decisions made?

Decisions are informed by the needs of students, faculty and staff who take courses delivered on the platform or build material with it. Direction is steered by the Core Product Development team, which meets monthly and includes representatives from any group contributing development, design or other skills. To join, contact Bill Rose, Product Owner.

What's with the 8-bit RPG characters?

The 8-bit look began as a Spring 2022 design challenge for four College of IST students, who built an experience that feels more like playing a video game than building a website. Each RPG-style character is unique to the user logging in, and the playful admin design helps separate authoring tasks from what site visitors see.
